Connect Home Ledger to Firebase

Home Ledger needs your free Firebase project configuration for private household synchronization. Open SETUP_HOME_LEDGER.md from the downloaded package and complete the steps, then update firebase-config.js in GitHub.

No payment card is required: use Firebase's Spark plan. Your website may be public, but household records are protected by sign-in and Firestore security rules.
